# F4: continuous low-pressure brush strokes

A controlled stylus stroke at pressure 0.1 on an owned 1000×1000 photo left visible gaps with fixed-distance sampling: the old stroke contained 13 dabs and 28 of 128 sampled centerline pixels had no mask coverage. In the actual photo UI, a gap remained at RGB 110 while a dab center changed to 114. Original offscreen and Cocoa QPA failures are preserved; these tests do not establish physical tablet-driver behavior.

The candidate samples more densely as pressure decreases, using a finite half-source-pixel spacing floor near zero. Mouse and constant full-pressure paths retain the prior sampling arithmetic, and existing stored strokes are not resampled. The original 4,096-dab per-stroke and 16,384-dab document limits, 8 Mi per-tile and 96 Mi operation work limits, 32 Mi source limit and shared 3 GiB reservation are unchanged. At the exact dab ceiling a finishing tail may be refused; the controller now shows the existing short-stroke explanation instead of silently leaving the document unchanged. Extremely low-pressure or long strokes can still be refused and subpixel coverage cannot guarantee continuous colored pixels.

Synthetic geometry checks covered two input packet densities, Paint/Erase, indexed and unindexed tiles, cancellation, schema round trips, 100 bit-exact full-pressure paths, 1,000 linear pressure paths and 400 turning and zero-pressure paths with subdivided inputs. On 7028×4688 and exact 8192×4096 synthetic geometry, a long path at pressure .005 is refused by the original per-stroke limit; at exact 32 Mi the same path at .01 is refused. These geometry preflights did not allocate an image, decode a RAW, render, or reserve the budget.

The private integrated photo UI regression exercised low-pressure continuity, one-step history, Undo/Redo, and the exact-cap release warning with no partial state. Six controlled QPA CPU/Metal flows used opaque/transparent images at 1× and opaque images at 2×; all original 17 captures plus five new captures, exact PNG16/ICC and the original 45-second limit were independently verified. Private evidence: `evidence/F4-low-pressure-combined-private-v2/seal.json` and `evidence/F4-low-pressure-limit-private-v1/seal.json`. The initial low-pressure and exact-cap UI failures, and the first candidate link selecting an older weak symbol, remain preserved. Controlled small images and simulated QPA events do not establish physical input or real-Sony behavior.

## Formal Root and committed CI

After CI321 was accepted, five candidate files were applied with a guard and the app, input probe, export verifier and Core tests were built serially. The existing and new targeted sampling tests passed. Formal Root controlled photo UI runs at 1× on CPU, Metal, transparent CPU and transparent Metal took 6131/6352/6107/6163 ms; the same four at 2× took 8411/8515/8140/8292 ms. All stayed within the original 45,000 ms, retained the prior pressure/eraser, history and exact PNG16/ICC checks, and independently verified 22 captures each. The new low-pressure stroke fills the old gap and supports Undo/Redo; the exact-cap finish displays its explanation without committing a partial edit.

On the public Sony ARW, the existing Root CPU/Metal pressure workflows completed their GUI phases in 10315/10600 ms and export plus independent reference in 40669.673/35513.377 ms. They retain 32,947,264 source, histogram and waveform pixels, exact PNG16 pixels and ICC, and peak shared reservations 1716323360/1720467584 bytes below the 3 GiB budget, under the unchanged 60,000 ms GUI and 120,000 ms export/reference gates. These are real-Sony regressions; they do not separately establish the new low-pressure scenario on real Sony. The 221 tested source files and four binaries remained unchanged throughout validation. Formal local evidence is sealed at `evidence/F4-low-pressure-continuity-root-local-v1/receipt.json`.

The source stamp recorded in these local reports is the pre-commit parent `8691c4d`. CI322 later completed with a Windows failure: the owned-image pressure GUI reached 45,110 ms against its original 45,000 ms deadline before finishing its export. Linux and macOS passed their individual audits, but the Windows package was skipped and the run as a whole was not accepted.
